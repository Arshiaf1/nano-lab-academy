🔌 BACKEND INTEGRATION

Your backend needs these endpoints:
└── POST /api/auth/register
└── POST /api/auth/login
└── POST /api/auth/refresh (optional)

Response format:
{
  "access_token": "eyJ...",
  "user": {
    "id": "uuid",
    "email": "user@example.com",
    "full_name": "John Doe",
    "role": "learner"
  }
}
